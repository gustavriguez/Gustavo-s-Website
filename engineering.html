IEEE USF leadership

Aug 2025 · Present

IEEE at the University of South Florida

Details

Lead IEEE USF’s lower-limb exoskeleton program and coordinate the student teams, resources, and research needed to develop an assistive prototype.

  • Coordinate mechanical design, electrical systems, controls, research, fabrication, testing, and safety. Manage design reviews, technical deliverables, purchasing, subsystem dependencies, and approximately $5,000 in development budget.
  • Guide a unilateral hip-and-knee assistance design using AK80-9 actuators, Teensy 4.1 controllers, Jetson Orin Nano, IMUs, and foot-contact sensing. Define requirements for joint alignment, power, sensing, human interfaces, and testing.
  • Use gait analysis, OpenSim comparisons, and MATLAB modeling to inform actuator sizing, assistance timing, torque targets, control strategy, and validation planning.
  • Organize workshops, recruitment, project demonstrations, technical meetings, and cross-team schedules. Coordinate career-fair and employer outreach, sponsor communication, and board-level events.
