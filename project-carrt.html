Research / Biomechanics / Embedded Systems

CARRT Research

A wearable ESP32/IMU sensor for rugby lineout research, designed for repeatable placement on the ankle and thigh and comparison with Vicon motion capture.

CONTRIBUTION & APPROACH

Support human-motion experiments through sensor setup, validation, data collection, post-processing, and data-quality review.

Designed the SolidWorks enclosure around the sensor PCB, balancing a compact footprint with secure strap mounting, protection of the electronics, and access for maintenance.

The early compact housing reduced the footprint from 80 × 60 mm to 48 × 36 mm. It added ventilation and adjustable strap slots so the sensor could mount without athletic tape or a sleeve.

The later battery-integrated configuration measures 62.17 × 58.00 × 16.50 mm. Its larger footprint accommodates the battery, USB-C and switch access, M2 fastening, and service access.

The enclosure revisions balance size with structural integrity: the housing, fastening, and strap interfaces need to hold the assembly securely during movement while allowing the electronics to be accessed. R9 is the assembly shown here.
