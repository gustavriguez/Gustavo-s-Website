Research / OpenSim / MATLAB / biomechanics

Gait comparison in OpenSim

OpenSim and MATLAB study of hip and knee gait mechanics, assistance torque, and electrical demand for a lower-limb exoskeleton.

CONTRIBUTION & APPROACH

Compared gait mechanics in OpenSim to examine how muscle weakness changes hip and knee demand across the walking cycle.

Used MATLAB to explore assistance torque, actuator current, and battery demand at different assistance levels.

The graphs are simulation results. The next step is to compare model predictions with motion-capture data and hardware measurements.
